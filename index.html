About Us

Built by students. Run by students.

Vol Motorsports is a student-led Formula SAE team focused on designing, manufacturing, and testing a high-performance race vehicle.

Our members work across engineering disciplines including chassis design, aerodynamics, powertrain, manufacturing, and testing. Every season, we build on the lessons from the previous car to improve performance, reliability, and the learning experience for the next generation of engineers.
